01 / THE PERSONAL JOURNEY

Two demanding paths.
One evolving journey.

2026

PhD completed.
A milestone built on years
of learning and persistence.

From the research lab
to the event site.

My PhD at IIT Bombay and my entrepreneurial journey grew alongside each other. Both asked for patience, responsibility, and a willingness to keep going when the next step was unclear.

In the lab, I worked on physics-based models of flow batteries and numerical methods for complex equations. Outside it, I was building eRentals — learning to manage customers, vendors, teams, payments, and the realities of delivering an event on time.

I began the business without external funding, an established team, or an office. As the demands grew, finding uninterrupted time for research became harder. There were periods when I wondered whether I would be able to finish my PhD.

“

Conversations with my supervisor, Prof. Venkat, helped me regain perspective. When the work felt unfinished, he helped me see how far I had already come.

I completed my PhD in May 2026. It is a milestone I share with the people who helped me reach it: my supervisor, my wife Fatima, my daughter Aayat Abdul Ali, and the friends and labmates who encouraged me through the difficult stretches.

The people behind the journey +

I am grateful to Prof. Venkatasailanathan Ramadesigan (Prof. Venkat) for his guidance and reassurance; to my wife Fatima for believing we could manage the challenges together; and to Aayat for bringing joy to the journey.

My thanks also go to Jadupati Nag, Shubhojit, Suman, Sandip Sawarkar, Keka Shah, and my labmates Mayur Bonkile, Akshay Desai, Ankush, Ritwik, and Manjunath.

2022BATTERY PROTOTYPE

An all-iron redox flow battery, from design to delivery

Designed, fabricated, and delivered a 5-watt prototype to the School of Energy Studies at Savitribai Phule Pune University. The delivery included two five-cell stacks — one assembled and one in kit form — along with supporting hardware and documentation.

Explore the work +

My work covered material selection, flow-field components, stack assembly, tanks, connectors, and tubing. It brought together modelling knowledge and the practical demands of building a usable research system.

FOUNDER & CEO · MUMBAI

Building a business.
Earning trust,
one event at a time.

eRentals began operations in January 2023, while I was pursuing my PhD. It grew from a hands-on effort to build a rental business in a fragmented, demanding market.

Today, my work spans event rentals, venue construction, and branding and fabrication across Mumbai and Navi Mumbai. It means bringing together materials, people, logistics, and deadlines so that a client’s plan can take shape on site.

Building without external funding taught me to make careful decisions, stay close to the work, and take responsibility when things become difficult. Research taught me to examine a problem thoroughly; entrepreneurship taught me to act while the problem is still unfolding.
